About 300 words, which is roughly 2 minutes at a calm, natural pace (about 145 words a minute). Each line pairs with an existing home-page animation or demo, so a screen recording of the site can be cut to match.

Tone: warm, confident, plain Irish English. One voice. Light music under; leave short pauses at the `/` marks.

## Wording rules

- Don't say "IPU approved", "certified" or "endorsed". A directory listing is not an endorsement.
- Don't say the PSI or IPU "requires" this product. Say "built for Irish pharmacies" and "the 2026 changes allow electronic registers".
- Every name and figure on screen must be fictional. The site's demos already are and carry a sample-data notice; keep that notice visible.
